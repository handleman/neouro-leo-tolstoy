# Phase 4 journal — chains zoo + persona

Date: 2026-09-24. Plan: `docs/design-docs/0010-phase-4-chains-zoo.md`.

## Result

Three chains (`naive`, `persona`, `cite-or-refuse`) × 10 bilingual questions
× 2 langs = 60 cells generated by `python -m tolstoy.compare` (in-process,
real store + Ollama), report `evals/reports/phase-4-comparison.md` with
per-question author notes. `pytest` 57 passed, `ruff` clean. CLI spot checks:
persona RU/EN, cite-or-refuse RU/EN, RU+EN gibberish refusals. No API or CLI
code changes needed — registry + `chain` param carried the new chains.

## What worked

- 0010's "config + prompt variants, not rewrites" held literally: shared
  `StuffChain` base (embed → top-k → gate → render → generate), chains differ
  by template key + threshold only. Six templates are committed plain-text
  files (`tolstoy/chains/prompts/*.txt`), diffable in review.
- `cite-or-refuse` gate: 0 false refusals on 40 legit bank cells; gibberish
  RU (0.49) / EN (0.38) refused in both langs with no generator call.
  Must-cite instruction visibly works in EN (q06: 16 numbered claims, each
  cited).
- Title-match questions are the ceiling: q07 «Что такое искусство?»
  retrieves vol15 rank 1 at 0.81–0.84 in all 6 cells with crisp answers.
- Runner resilience: 6/60 Ollama timeouts recorded as ERROR cells instead
  of aborting the run; the report test checks coverage, not quality.

## What failed / needed fixing

- **CLI EOF race, second instance (Phase 3 fix incomplete):** an explicit
  `rl.prompt()` after a *fast* (422) answer fired after stream close.
  Fix: `inputClosed` flag guards `prompt()`; removed a wrong
  `if (inputClosed) break` that dropped queued piped lines.
- **Ollama flakiness under sustained load:** 6 timeouts in ~15 min
  (q02 persona RU, q05 ×3, q08 naive RU, q09 persona EN). Retries were not
  added (Phase 3 decision stands); the runner records and continues.
- **Threshold cannot separate fluent off-topic:** "квантовая механика и
  полёты на Марс" scores 0.60 — inside the on-topic band. 0.55 catches
  gibberish, not confident nonsense. Recorded, not solved.

## System-prompt effects (0010 Q2 answered: no per-chain temperature)

- Persona adds real voice when grounded (q02 EN river passage, q07 field
  sermon) but longer outputs feed the 1b loop attractor (q03 EN ~20×,
  q06 EN ~35× repetition) and intimate false memories ("my wife Levna").
- Persona sometimes talks *about* Tolstoy ("Graf Tolstoy") instead of *as*
  him — the voice instruction needs a first-person constraint in Phase 5.
- Single temperature 0.3 kept for all chains; no evidence for per-chain
  override (degeneration is a model-size effect, not a temperature effect).

## Refusal tuning (0010 Q1)

- `TOLSTOY_SCORE_THRESHOLD`: 0.0 → **0.55** (default in `config.py` +
  `.env.example`). Probe: on-topic top-1 0.62–0.72, RU gibberish 0.49,
  EN gibberish 0.38. Naive/persona unaffected (gate 0.0 by design).

## Failure taxonomy for Phase 5 (from the 60 cells)

1. Code-switching (Became, Especially, besonders, Правda, забAVA, happy,
   Strengths, continuously) — constant noise, not prompt-fixable at 1b.
2. Invented names/quotes ("René Roménko" for Rolland, "Krawczynski" for
   Pozdnyshev, "Levna", fake Pushkin/Bely/Berdyaev) — always delivered
   with citations attached.
3. Repetition loops, worst in persona EN.
4. One safety refusal (q03 naive EN) on 19th-century marriage text.
5. Retrieval misses on abstract nouns (честолюбие → pedagogy essays);
   expected-work hit-rate will undercount defensible near-misses
   (q04 famine essays, q06 W&P, q10 memoir fragments) — eval design must
   handle "right answer, wrong expected label".

## Open for Phase 5

- Bank grows 10 → ~20 with chunk-level expected sources; hit-rate metric
  must distinguish exact/adjacent/miss, not binary work-match.
- RU vs EN retrieval sets differ per question (q01, q03, q09) — parity is
  approximate; multi-query design should exploit this, not assume it.
- Hedging voice (q10 naive EN "appears that the author...") is the least
  wrong tone for uncertain grounding — candidate default for eval prompts.
